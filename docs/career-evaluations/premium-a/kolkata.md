How this report reads the chart
- A field's natural significator is judged by its sign dignity, exactly as the engine judges a domain's karaka (karakaRules). Limits: The six fields and their significators are textbook associations (FIELDS), not an engine domain; the dignity reading is the engine's.
- A planet exalted or in its own sign in the D10 supports what it signifies professionally; debilitated in the D10, it weakens it. The engine applies this to the 10th lord (vargaRules); the report applies it to the other career planets. Limits: Friendly, neutral and enemy D10 signs are stated but not counted, as in the engine. Moolatrikona is not judged in the D10. The D10 depends on an exact birth time.
- A planet in a D10 angle or trine (1, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10) can express itself in professional life; in a D10 6th, 8th or 12th house it works under strain. This is the house rule the engine applies to period lords (planetCondition), read in the D10. Limits: Weak strength only; widely taught but applied here without a cited verse. Depends on the exact birth time.
- A sub-period lord placed 6th, 8th or 12th from the major-period lord is traditionally read as less harmonious; in an angle or trine from it, as more harmonious. Limits: Provisional: shown and interpreted with caution, never counted in a period's tone. Sign positions in the birth chart.
- Houses are compared only through rules that read every house alike. Left out: the rules whose direction depends on the kind of house (a malefic occupying or aspecting a house helps in the 3rd, 6th, 10th and 11th and strains elsewhere; a lord in a difficult house is not held against the 6th). They are classical and stay in the Career verdict, but comparing houses through them favours some by construction. Fields, which read different numbers of houses, also leave out the one-sided rules (a benefic occupant and Jupiter's aspect can only support), which otherwise rate most fields strong. Measured over 300 random synthetic charts (all engine rules → these): fields rated well supported per chart 2.4 → 2.9 with the one-sided rules removed (3.6 with them); Administration in the top tier 74% → 56%, Advisory 16% → 26%; employment favoured 51% → 31%, independent work 5% → 16%. Residual: Administration is still in the top tier more often than the others; not adjusted, since no further rule could be justified.
- A field is judged by the engine's resolveDomain over the counted evidence on its significators (D1 dignity, D10 dignity and D10 house) and its houses (the engine's house rules). Lordship of the 10th or the D10 Lagna is a link, not support: it makes the planet's condition matter, in whichever direction that condition points.
- Fields are ordered by that verdict, and within one verdict by the engine's balance (which only orders). Two neighbours are compared by the counted items only one of them draws on. Identical counted evidence means the chart cannot distinguish them; a shared verdict whose differences are all weak indicators means it does not clearly separate them.
- Employment (the 6th house of service) against independent work (the 7th of trade and partnership): each house's engine balance is read with the engine's verdict bands (r ≥ 0.25 supportive). One supported and the other not decides a lean; both supported is a mix; neither, or too little evidence (total weight under 3, i.e. less than one strong indicator), leaves it unresolved.
- For each career planet, the birth-chart reading is the engine's period-lord capacity (dignity and house). The D10 reading is its counted D10 items. D10 items only agreeing reinforce; only opposing contradict; both qualify; none leave it unresolved. Where the birth chart is neutral, a directional D10 qualifies it.
- A planet recurs as a strength (or strain) when at least two different counted items about it point the same way; where several do, the one with the greatest engine weight (strong 3, moderate 2, weak 1, as resolveDomain weighs) is named.
- A period's tone is the engine's period-lord capacity. What it activates is the houses its lord rules and occupies and the fields it signifies; Rahu and Ketu, owning no sign, also reach the fields of the lord of the sign they occupy (as commonly taught). The D10 standing and the relation to the major-period lord qualify the tone and never change it.

### Glossary

D10: the Dasamsa, a divisional chart traditionally read for profession.
Mahadasha: a major planetary period in the Vimshottari system.
Antardasha: a sub-period within a Mahadasha.
Sarvashtakavarga: a traditional tally of support for each house (about 28 points is average).
